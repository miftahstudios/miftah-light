Madih Light

Clear away the darkness, one beat at a time.

How to Play

  1. Move the orb. Swipe up, down, left or right (or use the arrow keys) to move one square.
  2. Move on the beat. Dots slide toward the ring at the bottom of the screen. Move when they meet it.
  3. Clear the shadows. Move into a shadow on the beat and your light bursts it. The closer to the beat, the better: Perfect, Great or Good.
  4. Keep your Light. A shadow that touches you takes 50 Light. Moves on the beat and cleared shadows give some back. Below 20, hits do half damage.
  5. Finish the song. Reach the end with Light left to complete it and open the next song.

Shadows move one square every beat and enter from the edges of the board.
